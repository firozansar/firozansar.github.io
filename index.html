03 / Experience & Education

Career Highlights

Select any milestone below to reveal full details.

Although I started as a mid-senior Software Engineer, I got promoted to Senior Software Engineer. Leading the work of designing and developing Android libraries written in Kotlin in the platform team—such as the User-Session library module encapsulating identification, authorization, and authentication for CTM app users—I also helped the Android Experience team integrate those libraries into the Meerkat Android app. Other notable work includes improving and automating CI/CD workflows, increasing test automation coverage, writing and publishing documentation using Dokka, code reviews, agile ceremonies, and cross-functional collaboration with engineers, designers, product managers, and SDETs.

As Android Tech Lead, I managed and mentored a team of developers while working hands-on. Apart from maintaining and adding functionalities to existing Stagecoach apps (Stagecoach UK Bus app, Oxford Tube app, etc.), I contributed to the vision and long-term technical strategy. The UK Bus app reached over a million downloads and ranked 13th in the Google Play UK Maps & Navigation category (Oct 2020). Notable contributions include implementing a live map displaying real-time bus movement, building the "busy bus" feature during COVID-19 for social distancing, and integrating Google Pay at the Google London office where I also gave a technical presentation on it.

Course modules included MSc Project, Enterprise Programming, Advanced Database System, Information System Management and Strategy, Object Oriented Programming, and Multimedia and Internet Application.
